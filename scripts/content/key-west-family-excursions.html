Family pacing for a compact call

Key West can be family-friendly when the plan stays short-distance: flat streets, shade breaks, and one or two stops rather than a packed landmark race.

What usually works

  • Walkable Old Town loops when docking supports them — walkability
  • Sit-and-ride overview options such as Conch Train context
  • Short Duval Street time with food stops — Duval guide
  • Deliberate rest and water breaks in heat

What to watch

  • Midday sun and queues at popular photo stops
  • Overlong offshore ideas that consume the whole call
  • Assuming every water trip suits every age without checking operator rules elsewhere
