Hop-on sightseeing as a planning idea

Trolley-style tours in Key West are typically framed as hop-on hop-off sightseeing through Old Town and landmark stops. For cruise visitors they can add flexibility: ride between areas, step off briefly, continue when ready.

Planning context only. This is not a ticket shop, timetable or operator comparison with prices.

How it differs from a pure walking day

Walking maximises flexibility on short distances. A trolley-style loop can reduce heat load and help with farther photo stops. Neither option replaces checking your docking point and return window — see the port guide.
