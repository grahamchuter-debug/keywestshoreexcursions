Questions about this page

How long do cruise ships usually stay in Key West?

Many calls are in a roughly half-day to long-day range, but exact hours vary by sailing. Always use your ship’s published times.

Can you walk from the Key West cruise port?

Often yes for Old Town, but distance depends on docking arrangement. See the walkability guide for Mallory Square and Outer Mole context.

Do I need a tour?

Many passengers explore independently on foot. Local sightseeing transport or water trips help when you want narration, less walking, or time on the water.

Is Dry Tortugas realistic on a cruise day?

Only when the call is long enough for a full-day offshore commitment with safe return margin. For many sailings it is not a routine fit.

Does this website take bookings?

No. It is an independent planning guide.
