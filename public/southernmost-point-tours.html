Southernmost Point — Cruise-Day Planning

The Southernmost Point buoy is a popular photo stop. Treat it as one optional landmark in a cruise day — not as a product you buy on this site.

Walk from portOne-day styles

A landmark, not a product

The Southernmost Point buoy is a popular photo stop at the south end of Old Town’s visitor map. Cruise passengers often combine it with a walking day, a pedicab or taxi, or a sightseeing loop.

Distance is docking-dependent. Do not copy a single walking time from “the cruise port.” Scope any timing to your berth, or choose a ride.

Cruise-day practicality

  • Expect queues on busy ship days
  • Heat and standing time matter as much as distance
  • It is optional — many excellent Key West days never reach it
